Role 04 · Credit adjudication

Risk analyst.
Beside the underwriter.

An expert coworker for the manual queue. Buy now pay later, credit cards, personal loans, lines of credit, cash advances, and payday. It applies your credit policy to the applications the decision engine already referred, and leaves a case the adjudicator can sign.

Hire this role See a case file

It does not replace the decision engine, set price, or originate. You remain the lender of record.

The queue

Applications a model already refused to finish.

New applications, limit increases, line reviews, recon requests, and the exceptions your grid sends to a person. Overnight and weekend included. The coworker does not rescore the book and does not hunt for declines the engine already approved.

Decision rights

Four calls. Nothing else.

Approve

Inside the amount, tenure, and verification your policy already allows. The case names the rule that passed.

Approve with conditions

Lower limit, shorter term, or a step-up you already offer: payroll connect, document, or a second pull. Not a new product.

Decline

A hard fail on your chart. Up to four principal reasons, copied from your adverse-action list. No invented reason.

Refer

Exception, thin file, missing verification, complaint, or anything outside the slice you agreed could close alone.

Shadow mode is recommendation only. Probation auto-closes only the classes that matched your adjudicator. Pricing, APR, origination, and sending the adverse-action notice stay with you.

Never alone

What it will not close.

Out of policy

  • Exceptions and one-off overrides
  • Amount, term, or geography your license does not cover
  • Payday rollover where your rule is a ban
  • A reason that is not on your adverse-action chart

Out of the slice

  • Missing income, identity, or payroll verification
  • Complaint, hardship, or vulnerable-customer flag
  • First-party fraud suspicion — handed to the fraud analyst
  • Anything the shadow week did not match

Protected characteristics are not inputs. The coworker uses the factors on your policy card and nothing adjacent. Inconsistent reasons are a scorecard miss, not a style choice.
